# Social Media Affordances & Engagement

Studies of how platform design, algorithmic curation and affordances (reactions, shares, ad-recruitment tools) shape user engagement and visibility of political and media content, including systematic reviews of Facebook reactions and analyses of what drives large-scale visibility on the platform.
