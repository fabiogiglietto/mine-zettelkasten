# Critical Analysis of AI Industry Imaginaries and Discourse

Studies examining how AI industry actors, media, and communities construct and circulate narratives, imaginaries, and framings (e.g., longtermism, techno-hagiography, 'quiet futuring', political-economy 'decoys') that shape public understanding and obscure or legitimize the underlying power structures of AI development.
