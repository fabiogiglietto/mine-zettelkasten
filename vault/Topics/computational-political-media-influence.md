# Computational Studies of Political Media and Advertising

Papers using large-scale computational or quasi-experimental methods to study political advertising, media capture, and ideological signaling on digital platforms, including causal analysis of ad effects, detection of covert advertiser influence on news coverage, and latent ideology estimation of political video content.
