# Health Misinformation Networks

Research mapping coordinated networks that circulate problematic health information, including COVID-19 fact-checking acceleration (MINE-FACTS) and cross-country studies in India and Nigeria funded by the Gates Foundation, examining how low-quality and ethnically/religiously inflected health content spreads via coordinated sharing.
