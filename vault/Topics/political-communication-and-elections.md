# Political Communication and Elections

Comparative and longitudinal study of social media dynamics around national elections (Italy 2018/2022, Germany 2021, Brazil post-Bolsonaro), covering partisan attention, affective community engagement, polarization, and hyperpartisan networks — the core empirical domain of the MINE programme.
