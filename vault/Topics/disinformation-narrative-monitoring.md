# Disinformation Narrative Monitoring

Tracking and characterizing the spread of disinformation narratives across Europe (e.g. war in Ukraine, LGBTQI+ rights, elections) through language models, dashboards and educational resources for fact-checkers, developed within the PROMPT project and related information operations case studies.
