# Italian Elections & News Ecosystem (MINE)

The MINE research program's core empirical focus: mapping Italian news media coverage, Facebook/Instagram interaction patterns, and political communication dynamics across Italian general and European elections since 2017, the institutional umbrella for the researcher's ongoing work.
