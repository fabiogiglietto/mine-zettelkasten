# Generative AI and Media Manipulation

Study of how generative AI (LLMs, synthetic images/video, deepfakes, conversational agents) is used to produce persuasive, deceptive or manipulative content, and how it reshapes disinformation tactics and countermeasures. Reflected in recent publications on synthetic gambling promotion, LLM-based dashboards, and the new 'IA Generativa e Media' course.
