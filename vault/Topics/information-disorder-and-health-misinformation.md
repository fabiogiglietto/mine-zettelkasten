# Information Disorder & Health Misinformation

Study of disinformation narratives, fact-checking support tools, and the circulation of problematic health and political information across contexts including COVID-19, the war in Ukraine, and health content in India and Nigeria, underpinning projects such as PROMPT and MINE-FACTS.
