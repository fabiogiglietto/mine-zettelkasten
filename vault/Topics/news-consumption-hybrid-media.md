# News Consumption in the Hybrid Media System

Studies of how news is produced, circulated and consumed across converging legacy and social media environments — insularity and cross-partisan sharing patterns, comparative newsfeed analysis across Europe, and the changing 'patchwork' of Italian media consumption.
