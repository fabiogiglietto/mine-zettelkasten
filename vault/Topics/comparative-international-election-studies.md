# Comparative International Election Studies

Cross-national analyses of coordinated behavior, political advertising, and partisan dynamics in non-Italian election contexts, including the 2021 German federal election, Brazilian post-Bolsonaro digital battlegrounds, and broader Latin American polarization studies—extending the researcher's coordinated-behavior methodology beyond Italy.
