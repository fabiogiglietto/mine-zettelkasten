# Social Media Research Methods & Ethics

Reflections on the affordances, limits and ethical implications of using social media platforms (Facebook, Twitter/X) as data sources for social science, including survey recruitment via ad platforms and interdisciplinary research ethics—an enduring methodological throughline underpinning the researcher's empirical work.
