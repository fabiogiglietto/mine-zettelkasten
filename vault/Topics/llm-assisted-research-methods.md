# LLM-Assisted Research Methods

Development and validation of workflows that integrate large language models into computational content analysis and annotation pipelines (e.g. LLMs-in-the-loop, judge-agent systems, embedding-based clustering), addressing reliability, validity and disagreement in applying LLMs to social-scientific coding tasks.
