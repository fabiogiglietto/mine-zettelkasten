# Generative AI for Content Analysis and Research Methods

Methodological research on applying large language models and generative AI to communication research tasks—validating LLMs-in-the-loop pipelines, comparing embedding models for clustering political news, and studying AI-generated persuasive content—paired with the newly launched 'IA Generativa e Media' course, marking a growing and current methodological focus.
