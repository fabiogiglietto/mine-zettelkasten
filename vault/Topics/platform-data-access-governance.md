# Platform Data Access & Governance

Research on the shifting infrastructure of platform-mediated research access — CrowdTangle's shutdown, Meta's URL Shares Dataset, API politics in the Digital Services Act era, and platform content-moderation policies (e.g. Meta's political content reduction) — and their consequences for studying social media.
