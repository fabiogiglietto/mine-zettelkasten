# Information Disorder & Disinformation Narratives

Monitoring and theorizing how false and misleading narratives (on topics such as the war in Ukraine, elections, and health) propagate within the hybrid media system, including the PROMPT project's narrative-tracking work and foundational taxonomy/definitional work on 'fake news' and misinformation.
