# Coordinated Inauthentic Behavior

Detection, monitoring and theorization of coordinated link-sharing and coordinated inauthentic networks on social media platforms, including the CooRnet/CLSB methodology, the VERA-AI Alert system, and studies of deceptive information operations (e.g. 'Zombie Internet', gambling promotion networks). This is the researcher's longest-running and most active methodological signature line.
