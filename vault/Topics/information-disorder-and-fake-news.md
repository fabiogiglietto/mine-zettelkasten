# Information Disorder and Fake News

Conceptual and empirical work on misinformation, disinformation and 'fake news' within hybrid media systems, including taxonomies of misleading information, PROMPT's narrative-monitoring dashboards, and reflections on the state of disinformation research and countermeasures.
