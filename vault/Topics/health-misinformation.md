# Health Misinformation & Fact-Checking

Research mapping coordinated networks circulating problematic health content (India, Nigeria) and tools to accelerate COVID-19 fact-checking, extending into trust and 'post-truth' dynamics around medical counter-publics. Reflects a sustained MINE sub-project line funded by the Gates Foundation and Italian MUR.
