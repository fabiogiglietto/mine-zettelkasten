# Platform Data Governance & Research Access

Work on social media platforms' data policies, API access under the Digital Services Act, and the consequences of platform decisions (CrowdTangle shutdown, Meta's political content reduction, the 100-share threshold) for research and for public visibility of political actors. Reflects his ongoing role as an independent expert for the European Commission's Article 40 DSA data-access process.
