# Information Disorder & Disinformation Narratives

Study of disinformation, misinformation and 'fake news' as phenomena within the hybrid media system, including narrative monitoring around crises such as COVID-19, the war in Ukraine, and elections. Forms the conceptual umbrella connecting MINE and EU-funded projects like PROMPT and vera.ai.
