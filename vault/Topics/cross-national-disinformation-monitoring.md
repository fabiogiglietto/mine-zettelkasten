Comparative international projects tracking coordinated behavior and political information campaigns beyond Italy — the 2021 German federal election, Brazilian post-Bolsonaro digital battlegrounds, and Latin American platform polarization studies — often conducted with international collaborators via vera.ai and PROMPT.
