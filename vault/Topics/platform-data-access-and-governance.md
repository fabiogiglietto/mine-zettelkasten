# Platform Data Access & Governance

Research and expert advisory work on social media research infrastructure, API access post-CrowdTangle, the Digital Services Act / ECAT oversight, and platform content-moderation policies (e.g. Meta's political content reduction); addresses the methodological and regulatory conditions under which platform research is possible.
