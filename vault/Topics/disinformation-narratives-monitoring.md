# Disinformation Narratives Monitoring

Monitoring and modeling the propagation of disinformation narratives across Europe on sensitive topics such as the war in Ukraine, LGBTQI+ rights, and elections, including dashboard and language-model tools built for fact-checkers and journalists (PROMPT project).
