Investigation of how generative AI is used to produce persuasive or deceptive content (e.g. AI-generated gambling promotion imagery, synthetic information operations) and how LLMs can be integrated into research pipelines for political discourse and content classification; also reflected in the researcher's new teaching on generative AI and media.
