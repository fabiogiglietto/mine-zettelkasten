# Platform Governance & Research Data Access

Research and policy engagement on the conditions, tools and APIs available to study social media platforms, including the effects of the Digital Services Act, Meta's political content reduction policy, the CrowdTangle shutdown, and the methodological consequences of data-access thresholds; tied to the researcher's role as a DSA/ECAT independent expert.
