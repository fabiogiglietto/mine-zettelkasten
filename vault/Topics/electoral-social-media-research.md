# Electoral Social Media Research

Country-specific studies of social media dynamics around elections (Italy 2018/2022, Germany 2021, Brazil post-Bolsonaro), examining coordinated networks, political advertising and hyperpartisan pages during campaigns — the empirical backbone of the MINE programme.
