# Generative AI in Media and Disinformation

Study of generative AI both as a vector for deceptive persuasion (synthetic visual content in coordinated gambling promotion) and as a research tool for content analysis (LLM-in-the-loop pipelines, embedding-model comparisons for clustering political news). Reflects a fast-growing agenda mirrored in the new 'IA Generativa e Media' course.
