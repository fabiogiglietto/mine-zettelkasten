# Information Disorder and Disinformation Narratives

Theoretical and empirical work on misleading information in hybrid media systems, including the taxonomy of 'fake news', trust and post-truth publics (Ciarlatani), and the PROMPT project's monitoring of disinformation narratives around war, elections, and LGBTQI+ issues. A foundational and continuously updated thematic pillar of the researcher's programme.
