# Trust beyond algorithms: What drives Italian audiences’ perceptions of search results and news produced with AI

> Fontes, G. S., & Splendore, S. (2026). Trust beyond algorithms: What drives Italian audiences’ perceptions of search results and news produced with AI. *European Journal of Communication*. https://doi.org/10.1177/02673231261473432
>
> [View paper](https://doi.org/10.1177/02673231261473432)

## Summary

This paper examines what shapes Italian audiences' trust in AI depending on where and how the technology is deployed in the information ecosystem. The authors draw a key distinction between two configurations: AI acting as an *intermediary* that produces automated search results, and AI *embedded within news organizations* to help produce journalism. Using a representative survey of Italian adults conducted in September 2025, they compare trust patterns across these contexts and find that automated search results are trusted more than AI-produced news. The paper argues that trust in AI is not a monolithic attitude but is contingent on the configuration in which the technology operates, and it highlights the growing prominence of automated search intermediaries in how people access and evaluate information.

## Key Contributions

- Provides empirical evidence on audience trust in AI across distinct use cases within the information ecosystem.
- Offers a comparative framing that separates AI as a search intermediary from AI embedded in news production.
- Contributes national-level (Italian) survey data on public perceptions of AI-mediated information, extending trust research beyond Anglophone contexts.

## Methods

- Representative survey of Italian adults fielded in September 2025.
- Comparative analysis of trust patterns across two AI configurations: AI producing automated search results versus AI embedded in news organizations.

## Findings

- AI-generated search results receive higher trust than AI-produced news among Italian audiences.
- Trust in AI is configuration-dependent rather than uniform, varying with the role AI plays in the information environment.
- The trust gap points to the increasingly significant position of automated search as an information intermediary (fuller detail truncated in the available abstract).
