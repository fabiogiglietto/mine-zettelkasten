# From \#StayWoke to “culture wars”: How social justice discourse is separately and synergistically politicized on twitter and YouTube

> Levin, S. H., Wang, R., Seo, J., Leong, Y. Y., Bennett, E., Yang, D., Yang, Z., Chen, M., Zhang, Y., & Suk, J. (2026). From \#StayWoke to “culture wars”: How social justice discourse is separately and synergistically politicized on twitter and YouTube. *Political Communication*, 1–25. https://doi.org/10.1080/10584609.2026.2717428
>
> [View paper](https://doi.org/10.1080/10584609.2026.2717428)

## Summary

This paper traces how social justice discourse—exemplified by the trajectory from the #StayWoke hashtag to broader "culture wars" framing—becomes politicized as it circulates across social media. The authors argue that politicization is not confined to a single platform but unfolds through two distinct dynamics: *separately*, within the distinct environments of Twitter and YouTube, and *synergistically*, through cross-platform interaction that amplifies the reframing of movement-oriented discourse into polarized terms. By centering cross-platform dynamics, the paper positions inter-platform circulation as essential to understanding contemporary political communication around social justice.

## Key Contributions

- Advances understanding of politicization as a cross-platform phenomenon rather than a within-platform one.
- Introduces a framework distinguishing **separate** (within-platform) from **synergistic** (cross-platform) politicization dynamics.
- Uses the #StayWoke-to-"culture wars" trajectory as an empirical case for tracking the transformation of social justice movements online.

## Methods

The study analyzes social media discourse across both Twitter and YouTube, comparing how the same underlying social justice discourse evolves in each environment and how the two platforms interact. (Detailed methodological specifics are not available from the abstract alone.)

## Findings

- Social justice discourse originating in movements like #StayWoke is transformed and reframed as it circulates online.
- Discourse shifts from movement-oriented framings (#StayWoke) toward polarized "culture wars" framings.
- Both within-platform and cross-platform (synergistic) processes contribute to this politicization, with cross-platform interaction amplifying the reframing.
