# COORNET: AN INTEGRATED APPROACH TO SURFACE PROBLEMATIC CONTENT, MALICIOUS ACTORS, AND COORDINATED NETWORKS

> Giglietto, F., Righetti, N., Rossi, L., & Marino, G. (2021). COORNET: AN INTEGRATED APPROACH TO SURFACE PROBLEMATIC CONTENT, MALICIOUS ACTORS, AND COORDINATED NETWORKS. *AoIR Selected Papers of Internet Research*. https://doi.org/10.5210/spir.v2021i0.12170
>
> [View paper](https://doi.org/10.5210/spir.v2021i0.12170)

## Summary

This paper introduces **CooRnet**, an R-based tool and integrated methodology for detecting **Coordinated Link Sharing Behaviour (CLSB)** on Facebook — the coordinated sharing of the same news article within an unusually short timeframe by networks of pages, groups, and verified public profiles. The authors argue that CLSB is a deliberate strategy to game Facebook's distribution algorithms and boost the reach of (often problematic) content. Rather than relying on content alone (debunking fake news) or static lists of malicious actors — both of which are laborious and blind to information operations that dynamically shift sources and accounts — they operationalize François's **Actors-Behaviors-Content (A-B-C)** framework as an *iterative cycle*: harmful content surfaces deceptive coordinated behavior, which identifies malicious actors, whose content then seeds the next iteration. The approach is demonstrated on COVID-19 problematic information in Italy during the first and second pandemic waves.

## Key Contributions

- **CooRnet**, an operational R package that translates the A-B-C framework into a reproducible, iterative detection cycle.
- A methodological alternative to content-only and static actor-list approaches, capable of adapting to the shifting sources, domains, and accounts of information operations.
- An empirical case study of coordinated problematic information around COVID-19 in Italy, enabling analysis at macro, meso, and micro levels.

## Methods

- Sourced **212 false/misleading Italian claims** (Jan 22–Aug 26, 2020) from the CoronaVirus Alliance database flagged by the International Fact-Checking Network (IFCN).
- Used CrowdTangle's *Search* to build a dataset of related news stories (**1,258 URLs**).
- Applied core CooRnet functions: `get_ctshares()` to collect Facebook posts sharing URLs within a week of publication; `get_coord_shares()` to flag coordinated shares (two actors sharing the same link within an unusually short window, with a configurable repetition threshold to guard against chance-based false positives); and `get_outputs()` to return coordinated entities, marked share data, a projected network graph, component summaries, and top shares/URLs.
- Used `draw_url_timeline_chart()` for interactive visualizations of cumulative coordinated vs. non-coordinated shares per URL.
- Ran **two iterations** of the A-B-C cycle, with the second seeded from posts by actors identified in the first, over October 2020.

## Findings

- First iteration: **13,271** CrowdTangle-tracked Facebook shares of the 1,258 URLs, identifying **152 coordinated actors**.
- Posts by these 152 actors over October 2020 produced **59,346 URLs** as input for the second iteration.
- Second iteration: **364,400** Facebook shares, identifying **344 coordinated entities** — demonstrating the amplifying power of the iterative approach.
- **COVID-19 skepticism, anti-vaccination, and anti-mask content** were prominent among the coordinately shared stories.
