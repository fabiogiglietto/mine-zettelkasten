## Summary

This paper investigates how far-right political actors deployed AI-generated multimedia content during their digital campaigns for the 2024 European Parliament elections, comparing practices across Instagram and X. It situates the analysis at the intersection of populist communication strategies and emerging generative AI technologies, arguing that the convergence of these two phenomena marks a notable development in contemporary political communication. The study treats synthetic images and other AI-produced media as tools that serve established populist messaging repertoires, while emphasising that platform affordances shape how such content is created and circulated.

## Key Contributions

- Provides an empirical case study of AI-generated content in far-right electoral communication within the European context.
- Connects existing scholarship on populist and far-right digital campaigning with the emerging phenomenon of generative AI in politics.
- Offers a comparative perspective across two major social media platforms with distinct affordances (Instagram and X).

## Methods

The study analyses political content posted on Instagram and X during the 2024 European Parliament election campaign, focusing on far-right actors' use of AI-generated multimedia. The abstract does not specify the full methodological detail (sampling, coding scheme, or corpus size), but the design is oriented toward a comparative platform analysis of synthetic media in campaigning.
