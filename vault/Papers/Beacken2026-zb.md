# Generative AI, propaganda, and digital authoritarianism: Comparative insights from six democratically weakened countries

> Beacken, G. D., Trauthig, I. K., & Woolley, S. C. (2026). Generative AI, propaganda, and digital authoritarianism: Comparative insights from six democratically weakened countries. *Big Data & Society*, *13*. https://doi.org/10.1177/20539517251410064
>
> [View paper](https://doi.org/10.1177/20539517251410064)

## Summary

This paper offers a comparative examination of how generative AI is being taken up for political propaganda in six democratically weakened countries — Bolivia, Hungary, India, Nigeria, Tunisia, and Turkey. Against techno-deterministic narratives that treat GenAI as a uniformly transformative force in politics, the authors argue that its propagandistic uses are shaped by local geographical, political, economic, and social structures. The study contributes to debates on digital authoritarianism by showing that elite adoption of GenAI is uneven, context-bound, and best understood through internationally grounded comparison rather than extrapolation from a handful of Western cases.

## Key Contributions

- One of the first cross-national comparative analyses of GenAI-enabled propaganda in weakening democracies.
- A structural, non-deterministic framework for thinking about how political elites deploy GenAI.
- Extension of the digital authoritarianism literature beyond single-country studies to a Global South–inclusive comparative frame.
- A response to calls for comparative research agendas linking AI and political communication.

## Methods

Qualitative comparative case study across six democratically weakened countries (Bolivia, Hungary, India, Nigeria, Tunisia, Turkey), situating observed GenAI propaganda practices within each country's geographic, political, economic, and social conditions.

## Findings

- GenAI adoption for propaganda varies significantly across the six countries rather than converging on a single pattern.
- Structural conditions — not just technological affordances — drive how and why elites use GenAI.
- Assumptions about the inevitability and uniform impact of GenAI propaganda are not borne out across cases.
